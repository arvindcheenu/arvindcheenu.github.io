How to play

  • Take turns adding one unit of matter to a cell. Each unit grows the body one stage.
  • Gravity: add to a body you own, or to an empty cell touching one. Cells you can reach glow in your color. Your first unit can go anywhere.
  • A cell's ring shows how much it holds, one segment per unit. Rim cells hold 2 or 3. Each ring toward the center of the well holds more, up to 7, so only central bodies reach the late stages.
  • Add one unit past the limit and the body goes supernova. It sends one unit into each touching cell and takes those cells over. Supernovae chain.
  • A body that holds 6 or 7 leaves a neutron star behind when it blows. You keep that cell.
  • With hints on, red dashes mark your bodies an opponent can take next turn, a pulsing outline marks the body they would ignite, and the preview shows what a move would do.
  • A solid outline marks the last move; thinner outlines mark the cells it captured.
  • Lose every cell and you've been swallowed. The last star left collapses into a black hole and wins.
